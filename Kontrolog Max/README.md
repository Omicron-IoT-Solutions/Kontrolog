## **📘 Overview:**

Here you will find instructions, manuals, schematics, packages, and examples to program your **Kontrolog Max** device, adapted to the functions that best suit your application.

The **Kontrolog Max** is a programmable controller based on **ESP32** technology, designed for remote monitoring and control of industrial processes that require high precision and safety. It is fully customizable and can be powered by AC or battery, and configured in either **No-Code** or **Full-Code** mode depending on your level of experience and needs.

## **⚙️ Technical highlights:**

### **🔌 Inputs and sensors:**

* 5 configurable inputs:

  * Analog: 4–20 mA, 0–10 V.
  * Temperature: NTC 10K – 3950.
  * Digital 1/0.
  * Pulse and frequency counter (input 5 up to 20 kHz).
  * Humidity and temperature sensors.
* 1 input for current transformer:

  * Available ranges: 0–30 A, 0–50 A, 0–100 A.

### **🔁 Digital I/O:**

* 3 GPIO ports configurable as inputs, outputs, or buses (I2C, SPI, UART).

### **🔋 Power supply:**

* Battery input: 6–12 VDC (with DC voltage measurement).
* Power source: 110–220 VAC (with AC voltage measurement).

### **💬 Communication:**

* RS-485 port with Modbus RTU protocol (master).
* Slot for communication module:

  * LoRaWAN™, Sigfox, or WiFi (depending on region and model).

### **🚨 Alarms and notifications:**

* Built-in visual and audible alarms.
* Support for remote alerts via:

  * SMS.
  * Voice messages.
  * Email.
  * Telegram.

### **⚡ Control outputs:**

* 5 relay-type outputs.

### **🧠 Embedded control functions:**

* Remote commands from the platform.
* ON/OFF control with hysteresis.
* Timer and alarms.
* Output proportional to sensor input.
* PID control.
* Event counter.

### **🖥️ Local interface:**

* 4.3” color touchscreen HMI for programming and data display.
* USB Type-C port for programming, simulation, and 5V power supply.

---

## **📦 Installation and power supply:**

* Connect terminals AC1 and AC2 to the power grid (110–220 VAC, 50–60 Hz).
* Alternatively, power the device using a 6–12 VDC external battery.

The system is delivered ready to use with the selected wireless communication modules and can be integrated with the Omicron IoT Solutions cloud platform for remote monitoring and control.
