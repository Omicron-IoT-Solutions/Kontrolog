## 📘 Overview:

Welcome to the **No-Code** branch of the Kontrolog project. This section is designed for users who want to get started with **Kontrolog Max** or **Kontrolog Mini** devices without the need to write any code.

Here you will find the necessary files to update your device and simulate its HMI touchscreen interface easily and quickly.

> ✅ **Ideal for all kinds of users: technicians, students, integrators, and enthusiasts.**

---

## 📦 What’s included in this branch?:

* 🔧 `.bin` files to upload the **precompiled firmware** to your device.
* 🖥️ `.hmi` files to simulate the HMI touchscreen on your computer.
* 📹 Links to video tutorials to guide you step by step through installation and usage.

> Each Kontrolog version (Mini and Max) has its own folder.

---

## 🛠️ How to use it?:

### 1. ⚙️ Upload the firmware

* Use the recommended **Flash Download Tool** to flash the `.bin` file to your Kontrolog device.
* Follow the corresponding video tutorial to avoid configuration mistakes.

### 2. 💻 Simulate the HMI screen

* Open the `.hmi` file in the **Nextion Editor** software.
* From there, you can simulate the touchscreen interface and configure basic parameters.
